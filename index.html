05Methodology

The three parts

Win points — your win rate scaled to a 40-point ceiling.

Contribution points — your mean contribution percentile, scaled to 45. Each game is judged by the metric that fits the champion's role, then ranked within that role.

Kill-participation points — your average kill participation, scaled to 15.

Contribution by archetype

Carry → share of team damage dealt.

Tank → share of team damage taken.

Enchanter → share of team healing + shielding.

A game only counts toward the archetype that best matched the champion played.

Eligibility

Players need ≥ 10 games in the window to receive a ranked score.

Fewer than 10 games are shown as provisional — listed but not ranked, because the sample is too thin to trust.

Within archetype boards, thin buckets are dimmed for the same reason.
